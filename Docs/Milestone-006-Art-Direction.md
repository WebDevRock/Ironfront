# Milestone 006 — Art Direction

**Date:** 2026-10-11 · **Status:** implemented this milestone (see [Milestone-006-Report.md](Milestone-006-Report.md))

All the art described here is generated by code in this repository (`Assets/Ironfront/Scripts/Editor/Art`). No third-party models, textures, fonts or sounds are used.

## 1. Audit of the existing visuals (Milestone 005 baseline)

The "before" screenshots are in `Docs/Screenshots/M006/before/`. They were captured by the same `ShowcaseRunner` shots used for the "after" set, so both sets have identical framing.

| Area | Finding | Severity |

## 2. Visual identity

**"Ironfront": stylised near-future military hardware on an arid steppe.**

- **Faceted hard-surface look.** Every model is flat-shaded, low-poly and chamfered: slab armour, sloped plates, bevelled edges. Lighting creates the detail, not textures. This suits procedural modelling, reads cleanly at RTS distance, and keeps one coherent style across vehicles, buildings and props.
- **Industrial realism, not sci-fi.** Concrete, steel, rubber, hazard stripes, warm sodium window lights. The "near future" shows in clean geometry, glowing optics and power coils, not in exotic shapes.
- **Readability first.** Silhouettes, team colour and a few emissive accents carry the information. Surface detail stays subtle (a shared mottled-panel texture) so it never competes with units.
- **Original design.** Nothing is modelled on Command & Conquer or any other franchise. The emblem is an original shield-and-chevron mark.

### Factions

Both factions field the same hardware, so balance stays symmetric. Identity comes from **livery**:

- **Cobalt (player):** 0.20, 0.45, 0.95.
- **Crimson (enemy):** 0.85, 0.15, 0.12.

These come from `MatchSetup` and are unchanged from earlier milestones, so the minimap and HUD still agree.

Livery is applied by `FactionColorizer` through property blocks to dedicated single-material renderers, so one model serves every faction:

| Entity | Livery parts |
|---|---|
| Tanks | Turret shell, fender stripes |
| Harvester | Cab roof, side stripes |
| HQ | Tower roof, roof band, banners, beacon |
| Refinery | Silo bands, roof edges |
| Power Plant | Tower bands, emissive power coils (glow in team colour) |
| Factory | Roof spine and side bands |

The ore colour is deliberately amber, distinct from the industrial ochre, and it is the only emissive warm-orange in the world.

## 4. Unit and building design principles

1. **One dominant silhouette cue per entity**, readable at 40–60 m:
   - **Light Tank:** low wedge hull, small octagonal turret, one long gun.
   - **Heavy Tank:** broad slab hull with skirt armour, big angular turret, twin guns.
   - **Harvester:** six wheels, open ore hopper with a visible load, front cutter drum.
   - **HQ:** stepped bastion, glazed command tower, rotating radar, helipad.
   - **Refinery:** sawtooth hall, two banded silos, intake hopper glowing with ore.
   - **Power Plant:** twin hyperboloid cooling towers that emit steam.
   - **Vehicle Factory:** ribbed arched hangar with a hazard-framed door and gantry crane.
2. **Team colour on top-facing surfaces**, so ownership is visible from the RTS camera angle.
3. **Models stay inside the gameplay footprint.** An EditMode test checks this for every structure. Fronts may reach up to 0.8 m onto the apron for door frames.
4. **Visual and gameplay are separate.** Models are child renderers with no colliders. The prefab roots keep exactly the same colliders, NavMesh agents and obstacles, and the same Health, CombatUnit and Building components and values. Named nodes that gameplay relies on are preserved (`TurretPivot`, `TurretPivot/Turret`, `Muzzle`, `Cargo`, `Crystals`, `SelectionIndicator`, `RallyMarker`).
5. **Polygon and draw-call budgets** (tested):
   - Tanks: under 2,500 triangles and under 9 renderers.
   - Harvester: under 3,000 triangles.
   - HQ: under 4,000 triangles.

## 5. Terrain and environment

- **Ground:** a 1024² painted map per scene, plus a tiled detail map:
  - dry grass and dirt variation;
  - lighter compacted-dirt roads with wheel ruts linking each base to its ore and the flanks;
  - gravel pads around each HQ, the natural building area;
  - rust-dark stains around ore fields;
  - darkened ground at the foot of obstacles;
  - a soft fade at the map edge.
  Roads are paint only, so they cannot affect navigation.
- **Beyond the map:** a skirt plane plus rows of faceted boulder ridges frame the 100 m square, so the camera never shows void. They are visual only, have no colliders and sit outside the camera bounds.
- **Obstacles:** same names, centres and box colliders as before. Each gets a styled mesh inside its box:
  - walls become segmented concrete barriers with buttresses and hazard ends;
  - blocks become bunker ruins;
  - towers become silo towers;
  - rocks become outcrops.

  The rebuilt NavMesh polygon data is byte-identical to Milestone 005. Only Unity's per-tile source hashes changed (16 bytes × 4 tiles), because each collider is now an unscaled box of the same size.
- **Props** (no colliders, merged into two meshes per map):
  - rubble (≤ 0.35 m) at obstacle feet;
  - shrubs and pebbles (≤ 0.42 m) in open ground, never on roads, base pads or ore fields;
  - barrels, crates, sandbags and tank traps only *outside* the playable square.

  Units therefore never visibly drive through anything taller than a shrub.

## 6. Lighting and atmosphere

- **Light:**
  - late-afternoon sun: warm (1.0, 0.9, 0.76), intensity 1.5, from 47° elevation;
  - cool trilight ambient: sky 0.50/0.56/0.66, ground 0.24/0.21/0.18;
  - light exponential haze (density 0.0032) that gives the backdrop depth without fogging the battlefield.
- **Post-processing** (one shared volume profile `Art/Ironfront_PostFX.asset`): ACES tonemapping, +0.35 exposure, +10 contrast, mild bloom (threshold 1.05), a slight warm white balance, a soft vignette and SMAA.
- **Shadows:** distance raised from 50 m to 100 m, because the camera can be 60 m from its focus point.

## 7. Combat effects

| Effect | Content |
|---|---|
| **Muzzle flash** | Star flare, flame tongues and propellant smoke along the barrel; barrel recoil. Heavy guns are 1.5× bigger. |
| **Shells** | Glowing tracers with HDR trails, oriented along their flight. |
| **Impacts** | Flash, sparks, smoke puff, ground dust; a metallic clank sound. |
| **Unit death** | Fireball, dark smoke, sparks, bouncing mesh debris, shockwave ring, scorch decal (30 s). A charred wreck copy burns for 7 s, then sinks away. |
| **Structure death** | Five staggered blasts across the footprint, debris shower, a five-second smoke and fire column, large scorch. |
| **Damage states** | Below 50 % health: smoke trail. Below 25 %: black smoke and flames. |
| **Construction** | Placed buildings rise out of the ground over 1.6 s with dust rings at start and finish. They are functional immediately, as before. |
| **Movement** | Hull pitch and roll from acceleration and turning, scrolling track treads, track dust, exhaust puffs, spinning harvester drum and ore sparkle. |
| **Ambient** | Rotating HQ radar, blinking beacon, chimney smoke, cooling-tower steam, ore glints. |

All particles are emitted into one shared world-space particle system per effect type, so spawning an effect creates no GameObjects. Emission is skipped for off-screen objects. Effects listen to gameplay events (`CombatUnit.Fired`, `Health.Died` and `Damaged`, harvester state); none of them read or write combat values.

## 8. Audio

Every sound is synthesised by `Core/Audio/SfxSynth` from deterministic recipes (noise, oscillators, filters, envelopes) and written to WAV files. There are 33 clips:

- cannon reports (2 light, 2 heavy);
- impacts (3), explosions (small, large) and structure collapse;
- radio-chirp acknowledgements for select, move and attack (a stylistic choice: no voice acting);
- placement, construction complete, unit ready and ore delivery;
- UI click, hover and error, an alert klaxon, victory and defeat stingers;
- a seamless wind loop, a seamless engine loop and three distant-battle rumbles.

`GameAudio` plays them as follows:

- Volume and pan follow on-screen position: an RTS camera hears what it looks at.
- A `VoiceLimiter` caps copies of the same sound (for example, at most 6 light-cannon voices) and enforces minimum intervals.
- A single engine loop scales with the number of moving vehicles in view, instead of one audio source per vehicle.
- Volumes are set by Master × category (Effects, Interface, Unit radio, Ambience) and persist in PlayerPrefs. They are adjustable from the main menu and the in-game menu.

## 9. UI styling

- Gunmetal panels with chamfered corners and a light rim (generated 9-slice sprites), signal-amber section headings with a thin rule, off-white text, and green, amber and red status colours.
- Text over the battlefield gets drop shadows.
- Build and production buttons carry **portrait icons rendered from the actual models**. The selection panel shows the portrait of the selected type.
- Credits use an ore-crystal glyph; power uses a bolt glyph and a load meter (green → amber above 85 % → red when low power).
- New screens:
  - **main menu:** a diorama of the real models, the emblem, Start, Options and Quit;
  - **pause menu** (F10 or the MENU button): Resume, Options, Restart, Main menu;
  - **audio options:** five sliders;
  - **restyled result screen:** emblem, card, Restart and Main menu buttons.
- The layout and the 1600×900 reference scaler from M005 are unchanged. The font is still Unity's built-in LegacyRuntime: no font files are imported.

## 10. Asset creation methods and limitations

| Method | Used for |
|---|---|
| `MeshKit` (own procedural modeller: chamfered boxes, tapered blocks, extrusions, lathes, tubes; flat-shaded, auto-oriented faces, one sub-mesh per material) | All vehicles, structures, obstacles, props, ore |
| `ArtLibrary` procedural textures (tileable value-noise fBm) | Panel-detail, tread, hazard, ground detail, particle sprites |
| `EnvironmentBuilder` painted ground maps | Per-scene terrain |
| `UiArtBuilder` raster shapes + preview-scene renders | UI sprites, glyphs, emblem, model icons |
| `SfxSynth` | All audio |

**Limitations (honest assessment):**

- This is coherent, stylised **programmer art**, not hand-authored production art. There are no UV-unwrapped textures, normal maps or baked AO. Edge wear and weathering are suggested only by the mottled detail texture.
- **Faction differentiation is livery only.** Both factions use identical models (balance symmetry). A second faction design language would need new models.
- **Animation is procedural transforms only:** recoil, turret yaw, hull tilt, spinning parts and the construction rise. There is no skeletal animation, the guns do not elevate, and the wheels are not individually animated.
- **Audio is synthesised.** It is convincing for weapons, explosions and UI; acknowledgements are radio chirps rather than voices. There is no music.
- **Tested in the Editor and a release build on one machine only** (RTX 3070-class GPU). Human judgement of the look, the mix and the readability at other resolutions is still required.

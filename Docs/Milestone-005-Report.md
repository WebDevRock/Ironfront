# Milestone 005 — Gameplay Quality, User Experience and Performance

**Date:** 2026-10-10 · **Baseline:** `14c8c89` (end of Milestone 004) · **Audit:** [Milestone-005-Audit.md](Milestone-005-Audit.md)

## Summary

No new factions, units or content were added. All Milestone 001–004 functionality is preserved, and every earlier test still passes.

- **Controls and combat:**
  - Units now return fire when shot from beyond their range.
  - Added control groups (Ctrl+1–9 / 1–9, press twice to centre the camera) and double-click to select all units of a type.
  - Formation spacing now follows unit size.
  - Fixed a bug where orders to a point inside a building were silently ignored (found by the balance study; it also affected the player).
- **Clarity:**
  - New minimap and notification feed (attacks, losses, construction, units ready, low power, depleted ore, insufficient credits), with Space jumping to the last alert.
  - Tooltips on every sidebar and order button.
  - F1 controls overlay.
  - Live placement hint (cost and validity next to the cursor).
  - Production queue shows % and seconds remaining.
  - Health bars flash when hit.
  - Match statistics on the victory/defeat screen.
  - The HUD scales with resolution and uses a consistent theme.
- **Performance:**
  - Profiler markers and a repeatable benchmark (25/50/100/200 units).
  - Pooled projectiles and effects; target search no longer allocates and has a cheap pre-filter; scans are staggered.
  - Two allocation regressions introduced by the new UI were measured and removed.
  - The release player was already fast (≥ 430 fps at 200 units in combat on this machine). Frame times are unchanged, and GC collections in combat dropped by about half.
- **Evidence:**
  - EditMode **162/162**, PlayMode **42/42**.
  - A new AI-vs-AI balance study over 20 matches: 10 seeds × both starting layouts.

## Phase 3 — User interface

All UI is generated by the scene builders (`RtsSandboxBuilder.CreateFeedbackUI`, `MatchSceneBuilder.CreateMatchFlow`). Colours come from `UI/UiTheme`.

**Minimap** (bottom left, `UI/Minimap`):
- Shows terrain obstacles, ore fields, structure footprints and units in faction colours. Selected units and structures are white.
- Draws the camera's view outline and a blinking ping at the latest alert.
- Left click/drag centres the camera; right click moves the selection. In attack-move mode, a left click issues an attack-move.
- Redrawn 5×/s into a 160 px texture, with no per-frame allocation.

**Notifications** (top centre, `UI/NotificationCenter`):
- Messages:
  - base under attack (structure named, red);
  - units or harvester under attack;
  - structure destroyed and enemy structure destroyed;
  - unit lost;
  - construction complete;
  - unit ready;
  - low power;
  - ore field depleted;
  - insufficient credits (both from placement and from production);
  - control-group assignment.
- Each kind is throttled (for example, "base under attack" at most once per 15 s).
- Messages with a location append "[Space]"; Space centres the camera there.
- Lines fade after 6 s; at most 5 are shown.

**Tooltips** (`UI/TooltipView`, `UI/TooltipTrigger`):
- Build buttons: cost, health, armour, power, plus status (locked by X / need $N more / click then place).
- Unit buttons: cost, build time, health, armour, speed, weapon, plus status.
- Queue slots: cancel and refund amount.
- Order buttons: what Attack-move, Stop and Hold do.

**Help** (`UI/HelpOverlay`): F1 toggles a two-column controls list. The hint "F1: controls · Space: last alert" sits above the minimap.

**Placement hint** (`UI/PlacementHint`): while placing, shows the building name and cost by the cursor, plus "Valid site" or the specific reason it's invalid (green or red).

**Production queue:** the head item shows `Heavy Tank 42% 6s` (plus "LOW POWER" when slowed). Queued items say "click to cancel".

**Damage feedback:** health bars flash white and enlarge for 0.15 s on each hit.

**Result screen:** a statistics table for you vs the enemy:
- units produced, lost and destroyed;
- structures built, lost and destroyed;
- ore harvested.

Kills are credited to the attacker's faction, including the killing blow.

**Scaling:** the HUD canvas now scales with screen size (reference 1600×900) instead of using constant pixels.

Screenshots taken during verification (release build at 1600×900, and the Editor) showed:
- the minimap with both bases, ore, obstacles and the view outline;
- the colour-coded notification feed;
- the aligned help overlay;
- the themed sidebar.

## Phase 4 — Performance

**Method:** `BenchmarkRunner` in the `RTS_Benchmark` scene. A player started with `-ironfrontBenchmark` writes JSON and quits. Two equal armies (half Light, half Heavy) are measured for 4 s idle and 8 s of combat, with the player's army selected so the HUD is live. Machine: Windows 11, RTX 3070-class GPU, vsync off, 1280×720 windowed.

### Regressions found and fixed in this phase

1. **Control-group polling allocated about 0.55 KB per frame.** Polling all ten groups each frame built a new "Group" + n action-name string per group per frame. This tripled GC collections in the first "after" run (for example 2 → 12 at 25-unit combat). The fix caches the action names; the development player is back to 0.05–0.23 KB/frame, the same as before the UI was added.
2. **Notifications formatted their message before throttling.** This happened on every hit taken by the player's units or base. The throttle is now checked first.

The minimap redraw also no longer allocates. Optimised only where measured: pathfinding (about 0.1 path requests per unit-second), AI frequency (0.000 ms) and physics queries were measured and left alone.

### AI-vs-AI balance (final code)

Setup:
- Normal vs Normal at 10× speed.
- Faction 2 is the scene's commander; faction 1's is added at runtime.
- Seeds 11, 23, 37, 51, 73, 89, 97, 101, 113 and 127, each played on `RTS_Match` (faction 1 south-west) and `RTS_Match_Swapped` (faction 1 north-east).

| Sample | Faction 1 wins | Faction 2 wins | Draws / timeouts |
|---|---|---|---|
| All 20 matches | 7 (35 %) | 13 (65 %) | 0 |
| Standard layout (F1 south-west) | 2 / 10 | 8 / 10 | 0 |
| Swapped layout (F1 north-east) | 5 / 10 | 5 / 10 | 0 |
| By start position | South-west 7 (35 %) | North-east 13 (65 %) | — |

- Matches lasted 279–559 s of game time (4.7–9.3 min).
- **This is not evidence of balance or imbalance.** 13/20 has a two-sided binomial p of about 0.26 against 50 %.
- The same seed doesn't give the same result: three replays of seed 37 on the standard layout produced F2, F1, F2. Frame-timing differences dominate, and the seed adds only extra variation.
- With 20 matches, a faction-2 effect (update order or scene-object order) can't be separated from a north-east position effect. The Milestone 004 sample (7 runs, 4–3) didn't show either.
- **Recommended follow-up:** at least 100 matches with both commanders created at runtime in random order, plus a human playtest of the map from both corners.
- Before the stuck-attacker fix, the same study (20 matches plus 3 replays) produced 2 timeouts. Diagnosing them led to the footprint fix.

## Requires independent human playtesting

- **Feel:**
  - whether retaliation, control groups and double-click selection feel responsive and predictable;
  - whether leashed retaliation sometimes pulls units out of position.
- **Readability:**
  - whether the minimap size, colours and point-filtered texture are readable;
  - whether there are too many or too few notifications, and whether the throttle windows are right;
  - whether the tooltip delay (0.35 s) and the help text are clear;
  - how the HUD looks at 1280×720, ultrawide and 4K (the scaler is set, but only 1280×720 and 1600×900 were seen).
- **Fun and pacing:** none of this can be judged from automated tests. Includes how predictable the AI is (audit #15) and match length (ore runs out at about minute 5–6).
- **Hitching:** the two single-frame stalls in one benchmark run, and behaviour on lower-spec hardware.
- **Balance:** see above; also the human-vs-AI difficulty of the Normal commander.

## Known limitations

- The minimap shows every enemy unit. There is no fog of war, and the camera can already see the whole map.
- Tooltips cover HUD buttons only, not units or structures in the world.
- Control groups only hold units, not structures. Double-click type selection uses the units visible on screen.
- WScript SendKeys can't drive the player's Input System, so keyboard shortcuts were verified in the Editor, not in the built player.
- The seed doesn't make AI-vs-AI deterministic.
- Audit items 15–17 were not addressed by design: AI strategy depth, ore duration, and right-clicking own structures.
